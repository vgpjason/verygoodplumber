Johnson County Inflow Program

A free initiative by Johnson County Wastewater to locate and remove private illicit stormwater connections that send rainwater into the sanitary sewer system.

Check Eligibility & Sign Up

Properties in the Johnson County Wastewater service area may qualify. A JoCo Inflow representative will evaluate your home at no out-of-pocket cost.

Schedule a Free Evaluation Official JoCo Inflow Page JCW Programs Overview

What the Program Does

The JoCo Inflow program finds and disconnects private sources of stormwater and groundwater that are illegally tied into the sanitary sewer. Common culprits include foundation drains, exterior walk-up stairwell or area drains, and sump pump discharge pipes that empty into the sewer instead of the yard or storm system.

Removing these connections keeps excess wet-weather flow out of the pipes, protects public infrastructure, and helps reduce basement sewer backups across Johnson County.
