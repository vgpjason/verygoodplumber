IPC 417.1 · Showers

Shower valves must be pressure-balanced, thermostatic, or combination type so sudden changes in supply pressure do not scald the user. The shower compartment or receptor must be waterproof, properly sloped to the drain, and large enough for safe use. Glass enclosures, when used, must meet safety-glazing requirements referenced by the building code.
