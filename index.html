About Very Good Plumber

Trusted, Experienced Residential Plumbing in Johnson County, Kansas.

Very Good Plumber is a trusted residential plumbing company serving homeowners throughout Johnson County, Kansas. Owned and operated by Jason Tope, we bring decades of hands-on expertise, honest advice, and quality craftsmanship to every plumbing problem we solve.

Decades of Experience You Can Count On

Jason has been working in the plumbing industry since 1989 and earned his status as a Master Plumber in 2007. With over 35 years of real-world experience, he possesses the knowledge needed to diagnose issues quickly and provide long-lasting, dependable solutions—saving you time, money, and frustration.

A Multi-Generational Family Tradition

Plumbing runs deep in the Tope family. Jason learned the trade from generations of skilled craftsmen before him, including his father, Stephen Tope, and his grandfather, Franklin Tope.

This family legacy isn't just about trade skills—it’s about values built on pride, honesty, and uncompromising workmanship.
